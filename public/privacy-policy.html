FaidaFarm

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

The short version

FaidaFarm helps smallholder farmers in Kenya keep farm records and see market prices, weather and buyers. To do that it needs to know who you are and what you grow. It does not sell your data, does not show advertising, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google To create your account and let you sign back in
Phone number and county, if you enter them To match you to nearby markets and buyers
Your crops, farm records and harvest dates To match prices, weather advice and alerts to what you actually grow
Questions and photographs you send to the farming assistant To answer them. See the section on the assistant below.
A notification registration for your phone To send price, weather and harvest alerts. Removed when you sign out.
Your farm's approximate coordinates, if recorded To fetch the local forecast

Your password is never sent to us. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which holds the password and gives our server only a short-lived token proving who you are.

What we do not collect

  • Your continuous or background location. The app never tracks where you go.
  • Your contacts, messages, call history or files.
  • Your photo library. A photograph reaches us only when you pick or take one yourself.
  • Payment or M-Pesa details. FaidaFarm is free and takes no payments.

The farming assistant and your photographs

When you ask the assistant a question, the question and any photograph you attach are sent to Google Gemini to produce an answer.

Photographs you send to the assistant are relayed to Google and are not stored on our servers. Please avoid photographing people, identity documents or anything else you would not want to share — a picture of the crop is all the assistant needs.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of providers to run the service:

ProviderWhat they handle
Google FirebaseSign-in, and delivery of notifications to your phone
Google GeminiAssistant questions and any attached photograph
RenderRuns the FaidaFarm server
NeonHosts the database holding your account and farm records
Open-MeteoWeather forecasts. Receives coordinates only, never your identity.

These providers process data on our instructions. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Our servers are outside Kenya, so your data is stored and processed abroad.

How long we keep it

Your account and farm records are kept until you ask us to delete them. Notification registrations are removed as soon as you sign out.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. Follow the steps at faidafarm-baa26.web.app/delete-account.
  • Turn off notifications at any time in your phone's settings, without losing anything else.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, by emailing the address below.

Children

FaidaFarm is meant for adults running a farm and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes, and how to reach us

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. Questions, requests and complaints all go to the same place:

info@growafricafutures.org
