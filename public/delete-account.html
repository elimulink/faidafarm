Delete your FaidaFarm account

How to ask us to remove your account and the data attached to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below from the email address you use to sign in to FaidaFarm. Sending it from that address is how we confirm the request is really yours.
  2. Put Delete my account in the subject line.
  3. If you signed in with a phone number rather than an email address, include that number in the message.

info@growafricafutures.org

We confirm by email once the account is gone. If you do not hear back within seven days, send the message again — it did not reach us.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account — name, email address, phone number, county Deleted, along with the sign-in credentials held by Firebase Authentication
Farm records — crops, harvests, plots Deleted
Questions and photographs sent to the farming assistant Deleted
Alerts, saved buyers and notification history Deleted
The registration that lets your phone receive notifications Deleted, so the phone stops receiving them
Market prices and weather records Kept. These describe markets and the weather rather than you, are not linked to your account, and stay available to other farmers.

How long it takes

Accounts are deleted within 30 days of the request. Copies may survive in encrypted backups for up to a further 90 days, after which they expire and are overwritten. Nothing in a backup is used for anything except restoring the service after a failure.

Deleting the app is not the same thing

Uninstalling FaidaFarm removes it from your phone but leaves your account on our servers, so you can sign in again later from any device. Use the request above if you want the account itself removed.
